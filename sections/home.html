Soft Matter & Statistical Physics Group

Prof. Awaneesh Singh · Department of Physics · IIT (BHU) Varanasi

We study the dynamic and structural properties of phase ordering in unstable, far-from-equilibrium systems — how nucleation, spinodal decomposition, late-stage growth, and coarsening shape soft, disordered materials.
